# Events

PEMRIX hosts and sponsors events to bring the community together.

## Upcoming events

Check the [community calendar](https://pemrix.com/events) for meetups, AMAs, and hackathons.

## Host a meetup

Community members can apply for small grants to host local PEMRIX meetups.

## Speaking and sponsorships

For conference partnerships or speaking requests, contact the community team through the Discord or forum.
