# Online communities

Connect with validators, developers, users, and contributors around the world.

## Official channels

- [Discord](https://discord.gg/pemrix) — developer and validator discussions
- [X / Twitter](https://x.com/pemrix) — announcements and updates
- [GitHub](https://github.com/pemrix) — protocol and tooling repositories
- [Forum](https://governance.pemrix.com) — governance and long-form discussion

## Regional communities

Find local language groups and regional events through the community Discord.

## Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Harassment and spam are not tolerated.
