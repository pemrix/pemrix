# Wallets

A PEMRIX wallet stores your private keys and lets you interact with the network. Wallets can be mobile, desktop, browser-based, or hardware devices.

## Self-custody

PEMRIX is self-custodial by design. Only you control your keys and assets. No central authority can freeze or seize funds.

## Supported wallets

- PEMRIX Wallet (official mobile and browser wallet)
- Hardware wallets with PEMRIX app support
- Command-line wallets for developers and validators

## Best practices

- Back up your recovery phrase offline and never share it.
- Verify addresses before sending large amounts.
- Use hardware wallets for significant holdings.
