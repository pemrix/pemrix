# PRX

PRX is the native asset of the PEMRIX network. It is used to pay transaction fees, secure the network through staking, and participate in governance.

## Uses of PRX

- **Transaction fees** — every operation on the network requires a small PRX fee.
- **Staking** — validators and delegators stake PRX to secure the chain and earn rewards.
- **Governance** — PRX holders vote on protocol upgrades and treasury decisions.
- **Payments** — merchants and users can settle in PRX or stable assets issued on PEMRIX.
