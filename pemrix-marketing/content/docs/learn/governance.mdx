# Governance

PEMRIX is governed by its community. PRX holders can propose and vote on protocol upgrades, parameter changes, and treasury allocations.

## Governance process

1. **Discussion** — ideas are discussed in community forums and working groups.
2. **Proposal** — a formal proposal is submitted on-chain.
3. **Voting** — PRX holders vote during the voting period.
4. **Execution** — approved proposals are executed automatically or by core contributors.

## Get involved

- Join the [Governance Forum](https://governance.pemrix.com)
- Participate in [community discussions](../community/online)
- Delegate your voting power to active delegates
