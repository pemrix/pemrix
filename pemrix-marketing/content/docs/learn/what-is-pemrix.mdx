# What is PEMRIX?

PEMRIX is an open blockchain network built to move value globally. It combines fast finality, low fees, and decentralized security with a user experience designed for real-world payments and AI-native applications.

## Why PEMRIX?

- **Open** — anyone can participate as a user, validator, or developer.
- **Fast** — transactions settle in seconds, not minutes.
- **Global** — send value anywhere with an internet connection.
- **Programmable** — smart contracts and AI agents can transact autonomously.

## The PEMRIX vision

We believe the future of money should be open, efficient, and accessible to everyone. PEMRIX is the infrastructure layer for that future.
