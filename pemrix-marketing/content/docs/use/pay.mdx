# Pay with PEMRIX

PEMRIX makes payments as simple as scanning a QR code.

## How it works

1. The merchant generates a payment request with an amount and address.
2. You scan the QR code with your PEMRIX Wallet.
3. Confirm the transaction.
4. The merchant receives the funds in seconds.

## Where to pay

A growing network of merchants, apps, and service providers accept PEMRIX payments. Look for the PEMRIX Pay badge at checkout.

## Fees

PEMRIX transaction fees are low and predictable, making it practical for everyday purchases.
