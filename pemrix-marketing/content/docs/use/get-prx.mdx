# Get PRX

PRX is available through exchanges, on-ramps, and direct wallet purchases.

## Exchanges

PRX is listed on several centralized and decentralized exchanges. Always verify the contract address before trading.

## Wallet on-ramp

The PEMRIX Wallet supports direct purchases with cards and bank transfers in supported regions.

## Earn PRX

- Run a validator or delegate to earn staking rewards.
- Provide liquidity on PEMRIX Exchange.
- Contribute to the ecosystem through grants and bounties.

## Store PRX safely

Once you have PRX, store it in a wallet you control. See [Wallets](../use/wallets) for options.
